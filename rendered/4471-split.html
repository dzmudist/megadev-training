The three problems

  1. Parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out flipped. Ticket: #1.
  2. booking-v3 hint says MM/DD/YYYY. People at day-first tenants type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it their way. A different bug: the hint is wrong. Ticket: #2.
  3. One-day shift. “Only June”, “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”. This looks like time zones or daylight saving, not a swapped day and month. Ticket: #3.

How the desk tells them apart

The cause depends on the tenant: their locale and the booking form they run. It does not depend on what intake tagged from the reporter’s browser; that tag is a guess and is wrong for a share of the hint reports. The table below groups by tenant and form.
